## 3-Minute Presentation Sequence

```text
[0:00 - 0:30]  The Hook & Accessible Live Recording
[0:30 - 1:15]  AI Meeting Intelligence & Decision Tracking
[1:15 - 2:00]  Ask the Meeting (RAG) & Voice Assistant
[2:00 - 2:30]  Productivity Analytics & PDF Report Export
[2:30 - 3:00]  Admin Governance, Usage Quotas & Offline Sync
```

### Step 1: Accessible Live Recording & Real-Time Captions (0:00 - 0:30)
1. Navigate to **Dashboard** and click **+ New Meeting**.
2. Click **Start Recording** to open the live recording dialog.
3. Highlight the live high-contrast caption stream, audio level visualizer, and accessible font controls designed for deaf and hard-of-hearing professionals.

### Step 2: AI Meeting Intelligence & Traceability (0:30 - 1:15)
1. Open the processed **Quarterly Strategy Review** meeting.
2. Demonstrate the **Executive Summary**, **Key Points**, **Decisions**, and prioritized **Action Items** (with assigned owners and deadlines).
3. Click a source citation chip to show instant highlighting and playback of the exact transcript timestamp.

### Step 3: "Ask the Meeting" & Voice Assistant (1:15 - 2:00)
1. Open the **Ask Meeting** tab.
2. Ask: *"What were the security concerns raised regarding cloud migration?"*
3. Show the instant, grounded answer with direct references to speaker quotes.
4. Click the **Voice Summary** button to demonstrate hands-free Text-to-Speech narration for visually impaired users.

### Step 4: Analytics, PDF Reports & Governance (2:00 - 3:00)
1. Switch to **Analytics** to show the Speaker Participation breakdown and Meeting Trends.
2. Switch to **Reports** and trigger a **1-Click Executive PDF Export**.
3. Open the **Admin Panel** to demonstrate SaaS quota meters, user role management, and tamper-resistant audit logs.
