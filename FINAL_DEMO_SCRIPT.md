# VOXMIND — FINAL LIVE DEMO SCRIPT

Step-by-step cue sheet for the 3-minute hackathon presentation.

---

| TIME | ACTION | WHAT TO SHOW | WHAT TO SAY | EXPECTED RESULT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00 - 0:20** | **The Hook** | Welcome slide or Dashboard overview | *"Over 1.5 billion people live with hearing challenges, and fast-moving teams lose hours weekly tracking missed meeting commitments. Pure audio collaboration leaves people behind."* | Judges understand problem immediately. |
| **0:20 - 0:35** | **Introduce VoxMind** | Login screen → Click Sign In | *"Meet VoxMind — an accessible meeting intelligence platform transforming speech into searchable, actionable knowledge."* | Navigates cleanly into workspace. |
| **0:35 - 0:45** | **Dashboard** | Organization Dashboard with metrics | *"Here is our multi-tenant workspace with upcoming meetings, recent action items, and team analytics."* | Clean Material 3 UI with zero overflow. |
| **0:45 - 1:15** | **Live Captions & Recording** | Click **New Meeting** → **Start Recording** | *"During live calls, VoxMind streams real-time captions with visual flash alerts and high-contrast themes for deaf and hard-of-hearing team members."* | Captions stream with color-coded speaker tags. |
| **1:15 - 1:30** | **Speaker Diarization** | Speaker cards with speaking times | *"We identify who is speaking, breaking down participation balance across all participants."* | Displays 4 speakers with duration percentages. |
| **1:30 - 1:45** | **Timestamped Transcript** | Transcript view with search bar | *"Every spoken second is indexed. You can search keywords and jump straight to audio timestamps."* | Lazy-loaded segments highlight search terms. |
| **1:45 - 2:00** | **AI Executive Summary** | Summary tab with key points | *"Post-meeting, our grounded AI extracts structured summaries with zero hallucinations."* | Key points and topics render cleanly. |
| **2:00 - 2:15** | **Decisions & Action Items** | Decision & Action cards with chips | *"Decisions and action items have explicit owners, deadlines, and clickable source citation chips."* | Clicking a citation chip seeks directly to audio. |
| **2:15 - 2:30** | **Ask the Meeting (RAG)** | Ask question: *"What was the final deadline?"* | *"Have questions days later? Ask the meeting directly. Our RAG engine responds with verified quotes."* | Instant answer: *"October 15, 2026"* with citation chip. |
| **2:30 - 2:45** | **Accessibility Narration** | Toggle **Voice Summary** player | *"For visually impaired users or hands-free commutes, VoxMind reads the full summary aloud with one click."* | Text-to-speech audio starts playing smoothly. |
| **2:45 - 2:55** | **1-Click PDF Report** | Click **Export PDF Report** | *"One click generates an executive PDF report ready for stakeholders."* | Instant PDF preview and download. |
| **2:55 - 3:00** | **Closing Impact** | Return to Dashboard | *"VoxMind turns conversations into accessible, actionable knowledge for everyone. Thank you!"* | Strong closing statement. |
