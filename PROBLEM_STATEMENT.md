# VOXMIND — THE PROBLEM STATEMENT

People with hearing, visual, and situational accessibility needs face substantial communication barriers during meetings, classrooms, interviews, and professional discussions when conventional collaboration systems depend heavily on pure audio communication. 

In fast-paced, multi-speaker meetings:
- **Deaf & Hard-of-Hearing Participants** struggle to keep up with who is speaking, missing critical context when live captioning is unavailable or lacks speaker attribution.
- **Visually Impaired Professionals** are left out of visual slide discussions and text-heavy chat threads without dedicated, hands-free voice summary narration.
- **Fast-Moving Teams** lose hours each week manually tracking decisions, deadlines, and action items, frequently suffering from missed commitments due to lack of source-verified traceability.
