# VOXMIND 3-MINUTE HACKATHON PRESENTATION SCRIPT

**Total Duration**: 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)  
**Presenter Flow**: Rapid Hook → Live Meeting Accessibility → AI Intelligence → Traceable Citations → PDF Export  

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Stage Directions

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Hook & Problem Statement
- **Screen**: Open Dashboard on projector.
- **Script**:
  > *"Every day, millions of meetings happen worldwide. But for team members with hearing or visual disabilities, as well as fast-moving distributed teams, vital decisions and action items slip through the cracks. We built **VoxMind** — an accessible, AI-powered meeting intelligence platform that turns spoken conversations into searchable, actionable knowledge."*

### [0:25 - 1:05] Live Recording & Accessible Captions
- **Screen**: Click **+ New Meeting** → **Start Recording**.
- **Script**:
  > *"During the live meeting, VoxMind streams real-time speech-to-text with multi-speaker diarization. For hearing-impaired participants, we provide high-contrast themes, large typography, and visual flash alerts so they never miss who is speaking."*

### [1:05 - 1:45] AI Meeting Intelligence & Source Traceability
- **Screen**: Click **Stop Recording** → Open processed meeting.
- **Script**:
  > *"Once the meeting concludes, our AI pipeline instantly generates structured intelligence: an Executive Summary, Key Decisions, and Prioritized Action Items with assigned owners and deadlines. And critically: zero hallucinations. Every single item has a clickable source citation chip that seeks the transcript and audio directly to the exact spoken second."*

### [1:45 - 2:20] "Ask the Meeting" (RAG) & Voice Assistant
- **Screen**: Switch to **Ask Meeting** tab → Ask question → Toggle **Voice Summary**.
- **Script**:
  > *"Have questions days later? Simply ask the meeting. Our conversational RAG answers directly from verified quotes. And for visually impaired professionals or hands-free commutes, VoxMind reads the full summary aloud with one click."*

### [2:20 - 3:00] Analytics, PDF Reports & Closing Impact
- **Screen**: Open **Reports** → Click **Export PDF Report**.
- **Script**:
  > *"Finally, one click generates a professional executive PDF report ready for stakeholders. VoxMind makes high-stakes meetings faster, smarter, and genuinely inclusive for everyone. Thank you!"*
